PNW Race Volunteer Opportunities: Be Part of the Spirit.

Our volunteers are the heartbeat of every WHY RACING EVENT. Join our crew, support amazing athletes, and experience the energy of race day from the inside.

Why Volunteer?

More than just race day support—it's a community experience that changes lives, starting with yours.

Join the Community

Meet passionate people and make lifelong friends while supporting the local PNW racing scene. Our volunteer crew is a family.

Earn Perks

Get a limited edition volunteer shirt, snacks, and "crew credit" towards future race entries. Our way of saying thank you.

Make an Impact

Your encouragement at a water station or course turn can be the difference for an athlete's biggest day. You'll feel it too.

Volunteer Roles

From the finish line to the far reaches of the course, we have a spot for you.

Course Marshal

Keep athletes safe and on track at key intersections. High energy and loud cheering required!

Registration

Be the first friendly face athletes see. Help with bib pickup and gear bags.

Aid Stations

The literal life-savers. Hand out water, electrolytes, and endless motivation.

Finish Line Crew

Place medals around necks and celebrate the incredible achievements of every finisher.

Ready to Volunteer?

Behind every personal record, every emotional finish, and every first-timer crossing the line — there's a volunteer who made it possible.
